# Issue #30: optional real HTTP product-card probe

\`inspectTenderProductCards(shortlist, requests, policy)\` is an owner-initiated read-only Apps Script adapter for up to five **exact HTTPS product cards**. It is not a search engine and does not supply its own merchant cards. It reads public HTML only with no cookies, Bearer headers, login, scraping bypass or redirect following; existing quota ledger counts each request. Calls require separate approved policy (\`approved=true\`, \`allowPublicProductFetch=true\`) and a fresh same-day candidate snapshot. Manufacturer/distributor hosts require additional exact host allowlist approval. Marketplace hosts are limited to fixed known market domains. No Apps Script publish or execution is performed in this PR.

\`parseTenderProductCard_\` extracts *one* schema.org JSON-LD Product and Offer with product name, brand/model, manufacturer part number/SKU, listed price/currency and stated availability. It rejects ambiguous identity, multiple offers, implausible/missing amount, foreign offer hosts, invalid price expiry, oversized HTML, absent structured data and ambiguous schema. HTTP 429, other status, non-HTML, late response and errors do not invent offers. The body SHA-256 is computed from exact received bytes; it is **not** a verified offer signature or supplier guarantee. No dynamic browser JavaScript is executed.

**Crucial limits:** a listed price is **not** a confirmed quotation. \`verifiedQuotes=0\`, \`evidenceStatus=UNREVIEWED\`, \`HOLD_NATIONAL\` and \`tsCompliance=UNVERIFIED\` persist. Even a JSON-LD \`InStock\` cannot establish required executable quantity, warehouse location, delivery to Kazakhstan or fulfillment period. Products missing merchant JSON-LD may return REVIEW; this is not proof of product nonexistence. Real suppliers and stock still require reviewed API/offer evidence. This adapter does not assert Terms of Service permission for any domain; check the actual merchant's requirements before enabling live execution.

No Sheets writes, paid OCR, automatic purchase, launch/scheduling, watermark, credentials or secret logging. CI uses synthetic HTML/HTTP, without live retailer requests. The final integrated pilot will need separate approval.
